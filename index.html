The climb

    How to play

    Each side gets a sum with four answers. A right answer pulls the rope toward your side, and a fast one pulls harder. A wrong answer slips you back and locks you out for a second. Drag the gold ribbon over your chalk line, or hold it on your side when time runs out.

    Keys: ASDF (Red) and JKL; (Blue), or tap.
